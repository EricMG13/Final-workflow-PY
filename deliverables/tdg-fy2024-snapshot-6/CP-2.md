### Downside mechanism, counterargument and monitoring

Demand or cost-recovery pressure first reduces earnings/conversion; repricing or
less effective protection can raise debt-service cash needs. Distributions and
other obligations then narrow the buffer and increase reliance on accessible
funds and financing. CP-2G supplies a tested conditional coverage warning, while
its cash-use omissions prevent a funding or default conclusion. Historical 2025
hedge/facility dates are past now; their actual outcomes were not verified.

The strongest contrary case is observed cash generation, the replacement/defense
business base, documented historical financing and supported rate mitigation.
It strengthens with realised price/cost recovery, reliable cash conversion,
restrained extraction, verified access and complete maturity funding. It weakens
with deteriorating conversion, unexpected uses, lost protection/access or
refinancing delay. Monitor actual cash uses, signed adjustments, price/volume,
hedge settlements, facility conditions and changed remediation estimates. No
rating, recovery, security choice or legal-capacity opinion follows here.
