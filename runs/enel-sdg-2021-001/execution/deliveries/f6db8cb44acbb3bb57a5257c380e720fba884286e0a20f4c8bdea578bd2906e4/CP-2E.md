### T2G.7 — credit implication

A real coupon incentive can consume cash when effective: on the stipulated
original principal, one fully stepped year adds EUR2.5m. It is not net hedged
group cost, a market-spread forecast or an established refinancing shortfall.
The initial coupon's zero is meaningful, but the issue discount and positive
reported yield prevent a claim of costless financing.

The contrary case is timely satisfaction of the complete condition, leaving the
initial coupon unchanged. No target-date certificate or notification outcome
has been admitted. Monitor the final defined KPI and process evidence rather
than treating June's shortfall as a realised miss. The programme distinguishes
the capacity-linked consequence from automatic default/repurchase; no review
of other possible default provisions or recovery protection is claimed.
