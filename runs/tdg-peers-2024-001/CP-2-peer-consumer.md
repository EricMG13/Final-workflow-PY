**Cash-quality challenge consumed.** Moog's CFO includes receivable-sale proceeds,
whereas TDG reports securitization borrowing in financing; CW customer funding
and Moog contract-advance reversals affect reported CFO. CW/Moog finance leases
sit outside raw borrowing captions and are explicitly added once; TDG/HEICO
already include theirs. The resulting bridge preserves both source debt and its
limited analyst comparison. These facts sharpen the previous concern about
cash-conversion definitions; they do not replace TDG's numbers with peer data.

**Contrary case and consequence.** TDG's reported cash-margin position is also
above this selected set in FY2023, so the FY2024 observation is not a newly
asserted one-year crossover. Sustained cash generation could support creditors
if retained and available. The prior synthesis documents actual shareholder
cash allocation and a later quarter's cash reduction despite positive OCF; those
separate dated facts remain relevant. No peer statistic proves the assumed
future repayment/restraint in the earlier conditional paths will occur.

**What to monitor next.** Trace actual cash uses and accessibility, recurring
earnings adjustments, working-capital financing and cash restrictions before
claiming debt-service or refinancing sufficiency. Request a targeted definition/
period bridge if a decision needs normalized peer leverage. Keep historical
September TDG, October HEICO, December CW and September 28 Moog periods separate;
the CW report was signed in February 2025, so this is not information available
at TDG's September 2024 close. No current agency outcome is inferred.
