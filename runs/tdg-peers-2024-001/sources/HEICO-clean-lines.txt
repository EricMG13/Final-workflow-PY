L2484: leads to a determination that it is more-likely-than-not that the estimated fair value of a reporting
L2485: unit is less than its carrying amount. If the Company elects to perform a qualitative assessment

L2487: perform a quantitative impairment test, otherwise no further analysis is required. The Company
L2488: may also may elect not to perform a qualitative assessment and, instead, proceed directly to a
L2489: quantitative impairment test. When performing the quantitative impairment test, the Company

L2491: impairment and an impairment loss is recognized in the amount by which the carrying value of a

L2493: are determined by using a weighted average of a market approach and an income approach.
