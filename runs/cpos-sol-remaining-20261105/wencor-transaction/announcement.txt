L6: HEICO CORPORATION AGREES TO MAKE MAJOR AND
L7: HIGHLY COMPLEMENTARY ACQUISITION
L8: HOLLYWOOD, FL
L9: and PEACHTREE CITY, GA / ACCESSWIRE / May 15, 2023 / HEICO Corporation (NYSE:HEI.A)(NYSE:HEI) today announced that it entered
L10: into an agreement to acquire Wencor Group ("Wencor") from affiliates of Warburg Pincus LLC and Wencor's management for
L11: $1.9 billion in cash and $150 million in HEICO Class A Common Stock1 to be paid at closing, or $2.05 billion in the
L12: aggregate.
L13: The transaction will be HEICO’s largest ever in purchase price,
L14: as well as revenues and income acquired. Wencor will become part of HEICO's Flight Support Group.
L15: HEICO stated that it expects the highly synergistic acquisition to
L16: be accretive to its earnings within the year following the closing. Further, HEICO anticipates it will continue to achieve its often-articulated
L17: growth objectives in the years following the closing.
L18: Founded in 1955, Wencor is a large commercial and military aircraft
L19: aftermarket company offering factory-new FAA-approved aircraft replacement parts, value-added distribution of high-use commercial &
L20: military aftermarket parts and aircraft & engine accessory component repair and overhaul services.
L21: Wencor is based in Peachtree City, GA and provides its parts and
L22: services internationally, employing approximately 1,000 team members in 19 facilities around the United States. HEICO currently employs
L23: approximately 9,000 Team Members at over 100 facilities worldwide. Wencor’s customers include airlines worldwide, aircraft maintenance
L24: repair and overhaul companies, military agencies and defense contractors.
L25: Wencor’s parts and repairs are found in hydraulic,
L26: pneumatic, electronic and electro-mechanical, cockpit and galley systems throughout numerous aircraft models and provide
L27: Wencor’s customers with significant cost savings.
L28: HEICO anticipates that Wencor will generate approximately $724 million
L29: and $153 million in adjusted proforma revenues and EBITDA, respectively, in calendar year 2023.2 Further, HEICO expects to
L30: receive estimated tax benefits of approximately $75 million that will be realized through fiscal year 2038. Including the estimated tax
L31: benefits and without accounting for any expected synergies, HEICO anticipates that its effective purchase price multiple will be approximately
L32: 12.9 times Wencor’s projected proforma adjusted EBITDA.
L33: Following the acquisition’s completion, HEICO anticipates its
L34: net debt-to-EBITDA leverage ratio will be below 3:1 and will return to its historical levels within roughly a year to eighteen months
L35: after the acquisition, before taking into account future acquisitions and other possible capital deployment activities.
L36: HEICO stated that it believes its Flight Support Group will achieve
L37: meaningful synergies from the acquisition, though it is not now providing details on those likely synergies. HEICO's Flight Support Group
L38: has successfully achieved significant financial and other synergies from acquisitions of other businesses over many years.
L39: Laurans A. Mendelson, HEICO's Chairman and Chief Executive Officer,
L40: along with Eric A. Mendelson, HEICO's Co-President and Chief Executive Officer of its Flight Support Group, together remarked, "Our
L41: Flight Support Group has for decades provided high-quality and reliable cost-saving products and services to the commercial aircraft and
L42: defense aftermarkets. The Wencor acquisition materially expands HEICO's aftermarket product offerings, enabling the combined company to
L43: offer even greater savings and capabilities to its customers, while expanding our new products and services development capacity. Wencor
L44: is a perfect and highly complementary fit with HEICO.
L45: Importantly, we look forward to welcoming Wencor's Team Members to
L46: the HEICO family and to working with Wencor's talented leadership team led by Shawn Trogdon, who will continue to lead the business."
L47: Shawn Trogdon, Wencor's Chief Executive Officer, remarked, “I
L48: am excited about the opportunity to combine HEICO and Wencor’s impressive teams who share the same culture and commitment to our
L49: customers, suppliers and employees. The unmatched combination will further accelerate growth, innovation, and development of highly reliable
L50: cost-saving solutions for our customers. I am proud of our team’s achievements to date and look forward to continuing our journey
L51: of growth with HEICO. I want to thank the Warburg Pincus team for their support and partnership that has helped enable our success.”
L52: “We are proud of the transformative growth of Wencor and significant
L53: expansion and deepening of capabilities during our ownership period. The company is very well positioned for future growth, and we wish
L54: Shawn and the entire Wencor team continued success. As growth investors, we feel privileged to have supported Wencor in building a leading
L55: design, repair provider and distributor of aftermarket aerospace components,” said Dan Zamlong, Managing Director, Warburg Pincus.
L56: Closing is subject to customary closing conditions, including, among
L57: other things, antitrust clearance. The parties anticipate closing the transaction by the end of calendar 2023.
L58: HEICO previously announced that it will hold its regular earnings-related
L59: conference call at 9:00 a.m. EST on Tuesday, May 23, 2023 following its earnings press release issuance the prior day. Dial-in and replay
L60: information is available on HEICO’s website.
L61: Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC acted as lead financial advisor to HEICO,
L62: with RBC Capital Markets LLC and Truist Securities also advising HEICO. In addition, Lazard and Moelis & Company advised HEICO. Paul,
L63: Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP and Akerman LLP acted as legal counsel to HEICO. Truist Securities has provided committed financing
L64: for the transaction.
L65: Citi
L66: acted as lead advisor and Jefferies acted as co-advisor to Wencor in the transaction. William Blair and Alderman & Company also
L67: served as financial advisors to Wencor. Cleary Gottlieb Steen and Hamilton acted as legal counsel to Wencor.
L68: HEICO has two classes of common stock traded on the NYSE. Both classes,
L69: the Class A Common Stock (HEI.A) and the Common Stock (HEI), are virtually identical in all economic respects. The only difference between
L70: the share classes is the voting rights. The Class A Common Stock (HEI.A) carries 1/10 vote per share and the Common Stock (HEI) carries
L71: one vote per share. The stock symbols for HEICO’s two classes of common stock on most websites are HEI.A and HEI. However, some
L72: websites change HEICO's Class A Common Stock trading symbol (HEI.A) to HEI/A or HEIa.

L74: About HEICO
L75: HEICO Corporation is engaged primarily in the design, production,
L76: servicing and distribution of products and services to certain niche segments of the aviation, defense, space, medical, telecommunications
L77: and electronics industries through its Hollywood, Florida-based Flight Support Group and its Miami, Florida-based Electronic Technologies
L78: Group. HEICO’s customers include a majority of the world’s airlines and overhaul shops, as well as numerous defense and space
L79: contractors and military agencies worldwide, in addition to medical, telecommunications and electronics equipment manufacturers. For
L80: more information about HEICO, please visit our website at www.heico.com.
L81: About Warburg Pincus
L82: Warburg Pincus LLC is a leading global growth investor. The firm
L83: has more than $80 billion in assets under management. The firm’s active portfolio of more than 250 companies is highly diversified
L84: by stage, sector, and geography. Warburg Pincus has been an active investor in the aerospace and defense sectors with current and former
L85: investments including Accelya, Aquila Air Capital, CAMP Systems, Consolidated Precision Products, Extant Aerospace, Inmarsat, Quest Global,
L86: TransDigm and Wencor Group. Founded in 1966, Warburg Pincus has raised 21 private equity and 2 real estate funds, which have invested
L87: more than $109 billion in over 1,000 companies in more than 40 countries. The firm is headquartered in New York with offices in Amsterdam,
L88: Beijing, Berlin, Hong Kong, Houston, London, Luxembourg, Mumbai, Mauritius, San Francisco, São Paulo, Shanghai, and Singapore.
L89: For more information, please visit www.warburgpincus.com. Follow us on LinkedIn.
