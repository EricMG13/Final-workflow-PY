### Overall structural view

**Fact → mechanism → consequence:** LMI's ICA recitals and individual acknowledgments describe multi-entity support for both classes; §§2.1 and 4.1 allocate shared collateral first to 1L, then 2L. That supports a qualitative lien-priority finding, not “structurally subordinated” notes by entity. No named non-guarantor value or stranded pool is established, and no route meets the entity-path, enabling-capacity and exposed-value test for a numeric leakage score. Overall leakage risk therefore remains **Not Scorable**, not a default medium score or average.

The strongest counterargument to weak 2L reach is §§2.3–2.4: reciprocal grants, good-faith cooperation and substantially matching documents resist unilateral collateral expansion. This argument would prevail economically if the underlying schedules show materially coextensive, perfected coverage, no material equity exceptions/elections and sufficient shared value after actual senior obligations. It cannot erase 1L control or conditional releases. Conversely, material excluded equity or a documented election would make collateral asymmetry economically important; entity names alone do not establish either.

**Conditional downside, not a historical event claim:** if a Company or mapped Guarantor default triggers 2L acceleration and notice before 1L discharge, the first legal break is loss of unilateral collateral enforcement under §3.1. Continuing operations or asset deterioration could then reduce collateral available while senior interest/fees and qualifying financing absorb proceeds. A 1L-led sale and §5.1 release could convert 2L support into a residual proceeds claim; refinancing under §5.6 can prolong senior priority. This is primarily an access/LGD and refinancing-control mechanism, not proof of increased PD or any recovery percentage. Observable tests are default/demand and received-notice dates, diligent enforcement/stay applications, proposed sale/release terms, actual claims, collateral valuations and DIP orders. No operational deterioration or default is established by this source.

Confidence is high for cited ICA mechanics, limited for independent entity support and insufficient for economic ranking/capacity. No computed confidence score or native acceptance is claimed: the supplied tools do not provide the selected method's confidence script. This does not change the explicit scope limitations.
