## Findings

### Definitions and ratio mechanics — P01–03

**Documentary Fact — P01:** Consolidated EBITDA starts with legally adjusted Consolidated Net Income, not reported EBITDA As Defined. Section 1.01 includes taxes, interest, noncash charges and broad transaction, integration, pension, earn-out, compensation-related and customer-arrangement adjustments, subject to the particular words of each limb and no duplication. Net-income exclusions themselves remove several gains/losses and purchase-accounting effects. Clause M concerns good-faith projected net cost savings from actions within the specified **24-month** windows; its **35% post-adjustment** cap applies to that clause's savings, not every EBITDA adjustment. [§1.01, L403–409, L432–441.]

**Analyst Interpretation:** Economic recurrence does not determine contractual admissibility. CP-1D's recurring-cost challenge therefore remains important to debt-service assessment but cannot rewrite the legal definition. Conversely, a contractually permitted adjustment is not cash available for interest or a forecast of realized savings. Clause M's time and percentage limits are real counterevidence to an unrestricted-addback characterization, but other limbs and the separate pro forma language remain important. Monitor the signed legal-to-reported reconciliation, action dates, savings net of realized benefits and double counting before allowing earnings adjustments to support a capacity conclusion.

**Documentary Fact — P02:** Legal FCCR is Consolidated EBITDA divided by Consolidated Fixed Charges over the **four full fiscal quarters ending before the transaction for which internal financial statements are available**. Pro forma treatment covers relevant incurrences/repayments and preferred-stock changes, proceeds applications, acquisitions, dispositions, investments, mergers and disposed operations through the Transaction Date, with the ordinary revolving working-capital exception. Third-party guarantees are treated as direct incurrences. Floating interest uses Transaction Date rates in the denominator, modified by qualifying swaps; acquisition earnings/interest are determined in good faith by a responsible officer, with reasonably determined operating-expense reductions permitted. [§1.01, L410–422.]

The controlling calculation perimeter is the **Company and its Restricted Subsidiaries**, not Holdings' entire GAAP consolidated group: Consolidated Net Income expressly aggregates the Company and its Restricted Subsidiaries, and Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated Interest Expense retain the relevant Person and its Restricted Subsidiaries. Unrestricted Subsidiaries are not automatically consolidated into these legal measures; the specified distribution and pro forma rules must be applied. [§1.01 L403, L424–432, L751, L815–819.] Fixed Charges include legal Consolidated Interest Expense and specified preferred dividends grossed up using the defined tax fraction, including the clause's deemed-dividend treatment. Legal interest includes cash and noncash net interest, capitalized interest, capital-lease interest and swap economics, but excludes specified issuance/financing fees, securitization charges and prepayment premiums. [§1.01, L423–431.] Thus **4173/1158 is not legal FCCR**: the reported earnings, cash-interest denominator, perimeter and transaction adjustments differ. No new issuer proxy is needed to demonstrate this incompatibility.

**Documentary Fact — P03:** GAAP is frozen as of December 14, 2010. Indebtedness is a bespoke obligation definition, while Consolidated Total Indebtedness uses principal and additional stock/contingent/lien-supported components with express exclusions. Secured-net debt deducts defined Unrestricted Cash, not automatically all Group cash. [§1.01, L522, L536–548, L442–452, L806–813.] The reported carrying-debt and broader-claims numerators cannot replace these definitions. Ordinary-course trade accounts payable and other accrued liabilities are excluded by the debt definition; customer funding float or supplier-finance programs require their own contractual classification. December contract liabilities 170 in CP-2D are not automatically debt or freely usable cash. Recourse and program documents are needed before concluding a financing arrangement falls outside the tests.

### Debt, leakage and cash-control mechanics — P04–08

**P04 debt permissions:** Section 4.09's ratio route requires FCCR **strictly greater than 2.0**, not at least 2.0; non-guarantor Restricted Subsidiary ratio debt has a separate USD400m outstanding ceiling. A Limited Condition Acquisition can lock the test to definitive-document signing. Permitted Indebtedness provides independent routes, including issue-date debt, Credit Facilities, purchase-money, refinancing, acquisition and foreign-subsidiary debt, with redivision/reclassification. [§4.09 L1294–1295; §1.01 L601–641.] These are not one aggregate free-and-clear allowance. The USD3100m facilities limb is reduced by specified securitization debt and asset-sale repayments/commitment reductions, while other facilities debt can rely on separately specified limbs. Purchase-money and general baskets each use greater-of USD250m/12% EBITDA formulas; the foreign debt limb uses USD450m/20%, with its combined preferred-stock condition. Acquisition debt can qualify through incremental ratio ability or improved FCCR. Refinancing includes amount and weighted-average-life conditions. No unused sum can be calculated without classification, usage and legal earnings records.

Additional Notes share a class with Initial Notes and are subject to §§4.09/4.12. A definition cross-reference at L349 mentions §4.03, whereas the operative issuance provisions explicitly require §4.09; retain that textual discrepancy, rather than silently repairing it. [§§2.02, 2.14 L915–916, L1012–1020.] No indenture-wide loan-style MFN pricing protection or sunset was identified. The missing credit agreement may have separate incremental/MFN terms; neither their presence nor absence is established here.

**P05 restricted payments:** The baseline captures dividends, equity repurchases, specified junior-debt retirements and non-permitted investments. It combines no-default conditions with a builder beginning at USD400m, 50% cumulative legal net income (minus 100% of losses), qualifying equity/contributions, conversions and specified investment returns, using the original historical dates. Exceptions include equity-funded transactions, compensation/equivalents, parent taxes/overhead, a greater-of USD75m/3% EBITDA basket and Excluded Contributions. [§4.07 L1200–1264.] The most material alternative permits **any RP** if no Default/Event of Default and pro forma FCCR **exceeds 2.0**, with rules for later loss of qualification and previously contracted payments. Classification and combination are expressly permitted. [L1265–1274.] This can preserve payout optionality despite a large historical distribution, but does not prove any particular payout used this route.

**P06 investments / USub:** Permitted Investments include restricted-group transfers, acquisitions and a general greater-of USD500m/25% Total Assets basket, plus a separate USub greater-of USD250m/12% EBITDA basket. Total Assets is the Company's restricted-group internal consolidated balance sheet, not automatically Group GAAP assets. [§1.01 L642–648, L795.] Designation requires Board/certificate steps, RP compliance and no recourse debt to the restricted group; outstanding investments are fair-valued and treated as RPs at designation. Redesignation requires ratio-debt ability and no default. [§1.01 L815–819; §4.07 L1275–1277.] Broad permission to transfer within the restricted group does not mean every recipient guarantees the Notes. The resulting leakage/recovery route is conditional, not a demonstrated IP drop-down or issuer intent.

**P07 liens:** Section 4.12 requires junior security unless the Initial Lien is Permitted. Exceptions include Bank Indebtedness, specified general/purchase-money debt, foreign assets and securitizations; ratio-debt liens require secured-net debt/EBITDA no more than **5.25x**, with Limited Condition Acquisition signing-date testing. [§4.12 L1336–1338; §1.01 L649–715.] Debt permission and lien permission must both be satisfied. These exceptions can add collateral-sharing or locally senior claims without establishing unlimited priming or a non-pro-rata exchange right. The current ICA is needed to determine practical priority and enforcement allocation.

**P08 asset sales and cash controls:** The Asset Sale definition excludes certain small transactions, internal transfers, USub equity, specified acquired assets and qualified securitization transfers. The latter has its own fair-value/75%-cash language with Purchase Money Notes deemed cash. [§1.01 L361–367.] For covered sales, fair value is required; the 75% cash/Cash Equivalents rule applies where related-sale consideration exceeds USD50m. Assumed liabilities, conversion within 180 days and designated noncash consideration up to greater-of USD150m/5% Total Assets can count as cash. [§4.10 L1296–1304.] Nominal cash compliance therefore need not equal immediately available cash proceeds.

Net Cash Proceeds allow specified deductions. Within 545 days, proceeds may repay permitted secured/non-guarantor debt or fund Productive Assets; binding reinvestment agreements have a 120-day completion extension and qualifying prior investments a 180-day lookback. Unapplied proceeds trigger a par offer, with USD40m aggregation, timing, pro rata sharing, and reset/residual-use rules. Asset swaps have additional collateral-replacement and proceeds controls. [§1.01 L583; §4.10 L1304–1319; §3.09 L1114–1149.] These controls rebut an unrestricted-disposal thesis but allow time and destination flexibility before Notes benefit. Affiliate transactions above USD20m have arm's-length conditions and above USD30m Board/certificate requirements, with material exceptions; upstream-payment restrictions likewise have extensive exceptions. [§§4.08, 4.11 L1284–1292, L1327–1335.] Neither clause certifies accessible subsidiary cash.

### Guarantees, security, remedies and control — P09–14

**P09 guarantees:** Holdings and Guarantors give joint-and-several payment/performance guarantees, subject to the liability limitation. Additional guarantees follow specified credit-facility borrower/guarantor status or at least USD200m of relevant Capital Markets Indebtedness within 20 Business Days. [§§10.01–10.02 L1886–1915; §4.17 L1372–1373.] Releases can follow permitted external sales, USub designation, immaterial status, release from the triggering other debt, or defeasance/discharge. The definition's 2.5% EBITDA and asset tests are conjunctive. [§1.01 L535; §10.07 L1935–1946.] Guarantees improve direct claim reach but are not permanent coverage of all consolidated assets. Named execution entities include TransDigm UK Holdings Limited and numerous US subsidiaries; their current value, jurisdiction and post-issue status require a separate entity map, not extrapolation from signatures.

**P10 collateral:** Collateral expressly excludes Excluded Collateral defined in the missing Security Agreement. Further-assurance and after-acquired-property duties are qualified by the security documents/ICA and specified credit-facility requirements; post-closing perfection efforts have a 90-day outside period. [§1.01 L389; §§12.03–12.06 L2181–2195.] Section 12.07 permits automatic release on guarantee release and other sale, loan-agent, USub, immateriality, defeasance and ICA pathways, with certificate/opinion protections for requested release instruments. [L2196–2221.] A broad grant cannot establish present perfection, valuation or lien continuity. Permitted releases are not equivalent to an amendment releasing substantially all collateral outside the documents.

**P11 defaults/remedies:** Interest has 30 days; principal, including tendered purchase payments, is due without that interest grace. Other covenants generally have 60 days after specified notice, reporting 180 days; §5.01 has notice but no waiting period. Cross-maturity/acceleration aggregates at least USD50m and excludes Securitization Entities; judgments above USD50m have their specified finality/60-day conditions. Collateral failures above USD50m have separate conditions; insolvency triggers also apply. [§6.01 L1470–1509.] These are not a universal cross-default to any A/R problem. No general ratio equity-cure right was identified, and this is not a maintenance-ratio instrument.

Trustee/25% holders may accelerate, with the Credit Facilities timing qualification; Company insolvency under the specified clauses accelerates automatically. Majority rescission/direction and indemnified, delayed suit mechanics are distinct from protected individual payment suits. [§§6.02–6.07 L1510–1553.] The form-note summary's broader insolvency phrasing at L2755 does not replace operative §6.02's Company-specific automatic acceleration; the indenture controls the note in a conflict [form note paragraph 4, L2706]. Enforcement of guarantees/collateral is expressly subject to intercreditor agreements, and holders cannot directly enforce Security Documents. [§12.01 L2107–2108; §12.08 L2222–2224.] No standstill length, turnover rule or purchase option is invented from these references.

**P12 amendments:** Ordinary amendments/waivers generally use a majority of outstanding principal voting as one class, including tender/exchange consents; affected-holder protections cover specified principal, maturity, interest, currency, payment suits, ranking and post-trigger CoC rights. Certain administrative, conforming, accession and already-permitted release changes need no holder consent. [§§9.01–9.02 L1795–1859; §2.09 L991–992.] The exact special fraction is **66 2/3%**, retained as **66^{2}⁄_{3}%** in L1860. It covers release of all/substantially all collateral outside the permitted documents or materially adverse ICA modifications. It is not the ordinary threshold, unanimity for all amendments, or a 3% threshold. Decimal-parser refusal of the markup is correct and does not alter its exact legal value.

**P13 suspension:** Both agencies' Investment Grade ratings and no Default trigger suspension of the enumerated covenants, including debt, RP, asset sales and additional guarantees; §4.12 liens and §4.15 CoC are not on that list. Reversion applies prospectively, with no retroactive default for suspension-period conduct, debt classification/grandfathering and specified guarantee catch-up. [§4.19 L1377–1420.] A later downgrade therefore does not simply unwind intervening leakage or debt. No actual suspension, rating event or notice history is established here.

**P14 CoC / continuity / exit:** The defined trigger includes specified all/substantially-all transfers, Company liquidation approval and more-than-50% voting ownership changes. The 101%-plus-accrued-interest put has notice, tender and payment conditions, prior repayment/consent requirements for specified other debt, and third-party-offer/redemption alternatives. [§1.01 L388; §4.15 L1343–1368.] Successor-assumption and conditional ratio/no-default protections also apply, with internal-transfer exceptions. [§5.01 L1427–1463.] Optional redemption has make-whole/equity-claw and scheduled call prices; there is no mandatory sinking fund. [§3.07–3.08 L1082–1111.] The form-note maturity remains **[•], 2033**; March/September interest dates do not establish its exact maturity month/day [L2650–2654].

### Overall legal view

Overall, the reviewed TransDigm notes package is **Aggressive, provisionally 4/5**, principally because earnings flexibility, multiple debt routes and the any-RP coverage route can combine to preserve shareholder-allocation and releveraging optionality. The main protections are a real junior-or-permitted lien restriction, direct guarantees, conditional sale/proceeds controls, payment-default remedies, protected individual payment/ranking rights and the special collateral/ICA consent threshold. They prevent a conclusion that the package permits every transaction or unlimited priming.

The causal downside is time-aware: demand/margin weakness or delayed integration first reduces actual cash earnings; claimed savings or legal adjustments can keep transaction tests stronger than cash economics; additional debt or distributions then increase service needs or reduce retained liquidity; renewal or payment dates finally crystallize funding pressure. Legal default is not necessarily the first observable break. The LGD pathway is different: additional collateral claims, non-guarantor/USub value migration, or permitted guarantee/lien releases can reduce reachable value even without an immediate payment default. A current entity/collateral map and the ICA, not a consolidated leverage proxy, determine the security-selection consequence.

The strongest countercase combines documented operating cash generation and historical financing execution upstream with the actual contractual constraints here. It would gain decisive weight with a certified restricted-group earnings/debt bridge, demonstrated savings realization, verified accessible cash, restrained allocation and a funded dated refinancing plan. That evidence could establish adequate service and renewal resources despite flexible permissions. Until then, flexible capacity is neither intent nor execution, and missing access evidence is not proof of distress. CP-2's conditional coverage concern and CP-2D's positive operating cash counterweight both remain valid without being relabelled covenant tests.
