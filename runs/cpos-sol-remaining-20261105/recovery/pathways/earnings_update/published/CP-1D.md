## Findings

### Credit read-through, contrary evidence and monitoring

Issuer-specific transmission is two-sided. Repeated acquisitions require integration cash and inventory purchase-accounting adjustments; stock/deferred compensation is ongoing remuneration even when current expense is noncash; serial refinancing creates repeated transaction costs. A53 and A142 establish these categories and their recurrence, while A52 explicitly acknowledges excluded integration cash. Accepting all adjustments as expendable costs would overstate retained debt-service resources. Ordinary EBITDA is a transparent sensitivity, not a validated quality floor: it retains gains and does not settle the mixed Other category. Q12's negative Other removes net income effects rather than simply adding costs; do not reverse its sign or manufacture a positive add-back.

The strongest contrary evidence is genuine reported operating improvement and positive cash generation: CP-1B records revenue 2,006 versus 1,789, As Defined EBITDA 1,061 versus 912 and OCF 752 versus 636. Noncash purchase-accounting charges can be separable from current operating cash, and a documented finite refinancing or integration event may merit normalisation despite recurrence of the category. That case would win only with component-level cash/noncash detail, completed actions, finite cost tails and observed post-integration cash margins—not merely the reconciliation. No prospective synergy or run-rate saving is supplied; none is invented.

Cash quality also matters more than the modest quantified debt uplift. CP-1B's current OCF includes an accrued-interest cash source of 201, compared with cash interest paid 162 and expense 378. Dividends/equivalents of (4,396) and repurchases (316) coexist with positive OCF and September-to-December cash contraction of (3,802). Do not infer that timing-supported quarterly conversion finances repeated extraction sustainably. Annual interest and tax bridge quantities differ from cash paid; the annual test below does not deduct them twice.

Causal downside: weaker defense/aftermarket demand or delayed acquisition integration first reduces operating absorption and cash margins; persistent compensation/integration costs then fail to disappear, while delayed interest/tax payments fall due. Further shareholder payouts would reduce the cash buffer before earnings alone signal distress. A fully drawn securitization increases refinancing dependence approaching its historical July 11, 2025 expiry (CP-1B); this is not evidence of a subsequent default or renewal. Monitor component-level realised integration costs, compensation settlements, organic margins, cash interest/taxes, restrictions and distributions. Pension funding is a counterweight: A135 says FY2025 expected funding is not material; gross accounting liability is not an immediate contribution call. Observable triggers are renewed integration charges after claimed completion, cash conversion weakening as accruals settle, remediation reserve revisions, and funding/lease commitments outside the quantified subtotal. No unsupported stress magnitude or haircut is applied.
