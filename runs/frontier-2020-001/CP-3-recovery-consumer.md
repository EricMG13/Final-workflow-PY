**Legal/LME overlay:** the source model's entity priority and deficiency treatment
are available; current priming, drop-down, uptier and unused baskets are not.
No adverse or benign capacity conclusion is filled into those gaps. The original
contract, final court treatment and individual entity records would control a
decision. These are comparisons of scenario recovery protection, not an invented
legal opinion or market compensation signal.

**Trade-off actually used:** 2L has much greater modeled downside sensitivity
than 1L across these cases, while HoldCo notes face structural dependence on
residual value and potentially different plan upside. That changes the questions
for security selection: what price compensates each risk, and which process/
claim basis is priced? It does not establish that the most protected instrument
is the most attractive investment. All preference labels remain Requires More
Work; the dependent market/RV assessment is blocked by missing inputs.

**Counterargument:** source high/low are not guaranteed bounds and liquidation
taxes are omitted. Operational/regulatory disruption could lower proceeds below
the source low case. Conversely an accepted/effective reorganisation may deliver
different consideration from liquidation. Therefore neither a zero 2L collateral
payout nor common-equity ownership alone settles the total economic recovery.
