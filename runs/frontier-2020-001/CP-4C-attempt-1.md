documented liquidation scenarios move the pledged-value marginal class from
the first-lien group to second-lien notes. Deficiency recovery at HoldCo then

gives Senior Notes the reorganised common equity, subject to dilution; these
are different value distributions and must not become one asserted fulcrum.
