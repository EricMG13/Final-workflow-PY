## Findings

### Identity, perimeter, dates and conventions

- **Consolidation:** Note 1 says the statements “include the accounts of TD Group and subsidiaries” and “Intercompany balances and transactions have been eliminated” ([S1 PDF p.110](S1-annual-report.pdf#page=110)). Page 3 defines the Company as TD Group together with TransDigm Inc. and its wholly-owned and majority-owned subsidiaries with a controlling interest. These are consolidated group statements, not TransDigm Inc. standalone, a restricted-group covenant perimeter, or guarantor-only accounts. Noncontrolling interests are separately presented on the primary statements.
- **Accounting basis:** Note 1 states “prepared in conformity with U.S. GAAP” and notes prior-year reclassifications to current presentation, “none of which are material” (p.110). Preserve the report's comparative presentation; CP-0 has not reconstructed original prior-year filings.
- **Period policy:** “References to ‘fiscal year’ mean the year ending or ended September 30”; FY2024 is “the period from October 1, 2023 to September 30, 2024” ([p.3](S1-annual-report.pdf#page=3)). Balance sheets are instants at September 30, 2024 and 2023 (p.105). Income, comprehensive income, equity movements and cash flows cover fiscal years ended September 30, 2024, 2023 and 2022 (pp.106–109). The equity statement also carries the opening September 30, 2021 balance; that is not an extra annual income-statement column.
- **Money versus shares:** Balance sheet and equity statement headers say “Amounts in millions, except share amounts” (pp.105,108); income says “Amounts in millions, except per share amounts” (p.106); comprehensive income and cash flows say “Amounts in millions” (pp.107,109). Monetary reporting is U.S. dollars: Note 1 explicitly describes translation into “U.S. dollars” (p.114), and statements carry dollar signs. Thus preserve USD millions for money, actual share counts where excepted, millions of weighted-average shares on the income statement, and USD per share for EPS and dividends. Note 4 labels its EPS computation “in millions, except per share data” (p.122). Do not apply one blanket multiplier to a whole statement.
- **EPS and attribution:** Note 1 specifies the “two-class method” (p.114); Note 4 separates common shares and vested options deemed participating securities (p.122). Preserve distinctions between consolidated net income, TD Group-attributable income and income applicable to common stockholders; do not silently substitute one for another.
- **As-of separation:** The report and audit signatures postdate September 30, 2024. Page 131 explicitly labels an October 2024 securitization draw “Subsequent Event”; p.146 labels the October special-dividend payment “Subsequent Event.” These disclosures may be tagged as subsequent events, not rolled into September 30 balances or FY2024 cash flows. The declaration/accrual versus payment distinction must survive downstream extraction.
