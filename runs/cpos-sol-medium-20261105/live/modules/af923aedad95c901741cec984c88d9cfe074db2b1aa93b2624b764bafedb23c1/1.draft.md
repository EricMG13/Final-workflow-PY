### Financial read-through from retained evidence

**Operating:** reported annual revenue and operating profit rose in FY2024 versus FY2023 (I01/I06); calculated increases are 1355 and 608 USD millions respectively (K15–K16). Gross margin improved while operating margin was broadly stable at the displayed annual ratios (K02–K03). The complete below-gross-profit expense block is S&A plus intangible amortization (I04–I05), not total D&A; K01/R01 reconcile it. No organic-growth, price/volume or acquisition-causality allocation is made. Acquisition-date inclusion and preliminary purchase accounting make that restraint material (Ev1–Ev3, S1 PDF pp.112, 115–120). EBITDA As Defined exceeds ordinary EBITDA through issuer adjustments (K13, A06–A09); these include cash integration costs and compensation, so an add-back is not evidence of cash availability (PDF pp.52–53).

**Cash conversion:** FY2024 CFO increased by 670 USD millions (K17) and CFO/EBITDA As Defined improved (K05). The acquisition-adjusted signed asset/liability cash changes remained a use of cash, but smaller than FY2023 (K14; C15–C21). This is a cash-flow observation, not evidence that all year-end working-capital movement was organic. CFO less capex increased (K06), but C24 acquisitions and C28 distributions are outside that proxy; it is not discretionary cash after all strategic and shareholder outlays. Non-cash compensation C10 and the mismatch between tax expense I11 and cash taxes C44 remain relevant quality distinctions; neither is normalized away.

**Funding/liquidity:** both carrying debt and cash increased (K07, B01); net carrying debt/EBITDA As Defined fell while gross carrying leverage rose (K09–K10). Operating-profit/net-interest coverage improved (K11), but does not cover principal amortization or gross cash debt service. Cash plus available revolver (K12) is a gross snapshot, not freely distributable liquidity: B14/Ev5 show a large declared unpaid dividend, and Ev6 records its subsequent settlement. The disclosed debt-financed September distribution (Ev4) and historical acquisition funding (Ev1) establish issuer-reported uses; they do not establish legal distribution capacity. Finance leases are in reported debt and operating leases sit in B15/B19 (L11–L17); contractual lease payments include interest and must not be equated to principal. Maturities D29 concentrate larger scheduled amounts in later fiscal years, while securitization D25 is short-term and separate. No refinancing assurance, covenant headroom or credit recommendation follows from these annual observations.
