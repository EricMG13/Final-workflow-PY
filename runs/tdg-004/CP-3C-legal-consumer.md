facility, but governs the 2033 secured notes. It cannot establish that the
separate July 11, 2025 facility was extended, that all commitment is drawable,

CP-4 also prevents treating a computed annual EBITDA/cash-interest ratio as
permission to refinance or distribute. The legal fixed-charge ratio has its

a maintenance covenant. Other debt routes may exist, but their bases, conditions,
classification and usage require evidence. Permission, remaining capacity,
market access and actual cash are four different conclusions.
