### Definitions and ratio mechanics — P01–03

The **“greater than 2.0 to 1.0”** incurrence condition is strict. Equality does
not satisfy that route; failing the route does not itself establish a default
or eliminate separately permitted debt. The legal numerator/denominator,
four-quarter window and pro-forma transaction treatment must be established
before a numeric comparison. Some grower baskets separately specify a filing/
period lag; that is not automatically the ratio test's timing rule.

CP-1's annual EBITDA As Defined and cash interest are not a verified legal fixed-
charge ratio. D1's adjustments, preferred-dividend treatment, hedge-sensitive
interest/pro-forma rules and Company/Restricted Subsidiary perimeter require a
bridge. The document predates the September fiscal year end: later annual values
cannot silently become the issuance-date tested period. Metric equivalence and
current headroom are **Not Calculable** here.

P02's clause-M cost-savings provision has a 24-month action window and a 35%
cap measured after its own adjustment. The cap is not 35% of an unadjusted base
and is not automatically a cap on every other adjustment. Calling all synergies
uncapped would discard a real protection; calling the cap a universal earnings-
quality safeguard would also overstate it. **Inference:** adjustments can expand
permission without equivalent cash. Monitor eligibility, timing, the inclusive
base and separate pro-forma additions; do not equate legal EBITDA with sustainable
cash or independently verified earnings.

### Debt, leakage and cash-control mechanics — P04–08

**Inference:** separate debt routes and reclassification can preserve financing
options after one route tightens. They can also increase competing claims. A
ratio permission is not a funded facility, unused basket or assured refinancing.
Current balances, legal bases, prior usage and other governing agreements are
required before capacity is combined. The indenture alone does not establish
credit-agreement incremental/MFN terms or a lawful priming transaction.

P05's ratio-based RP permission retains its default and pro-forma conditions,
with a continuation rule for qualifying contractual commitments. **Inference:**
cash can become committed before a later deterioration, which matters for
liquidity. Do not treat that as proof a particular distribution qualified, or
that all payments remain permitted after deterioration. CP-1A's documented
distribution is an actual cash-policy signal, not its legal basket allocation.

P06's investment/designation routes require permission, valuation and other
conditions; designation charges the specified investment value. **Inference:**
potential value migration warrants a route/usage check. It is neither an actual
designation nor proof of an unconstrained drop-down. No specific non-guarantor
asset value, actual transfer or resulting numerical loss is inferred.

P07/08 constrain some liens and asset dispositions but include exceptions and
reinvestment options. **Inference:** apparent collateral or sale proceeds may
not translate into an immediate noteholder cash sweep. The protective contrary
evidence is the actual priority/consideration/offer framework and its conditions.
Follow the definitions and trace actual proceeds, obligations and permitted
application before claiming an erosion or an offer is due. The A/R facility's
separate document is required for its actual access/collateral conditions.

### Guarantees, security, remedies and control — P09–14

P09/10 supply real guarantee/security protections and conditional release paths.
**Inference:** legal status and collateral access can change with a permitted
transaction; static consolidated assets do not prove reachable secured value.
Security perfection, exclusions and the full entity-by-entity map require the
dependent documents. This interpretation is not CP-4B's complete priority map.

P11 gives interest nonpayment a 30-day condition, while principal is due under
its stated payment terms. Other covenant defaults have notice/cure mechanics
with distinct general/reporting periods. Cross-payment/acceleration has a
$50 million threshold and expressly excludes a Securitization Entity from the
named subsidiary scope; do not assume an obligor qualifies for that exclusion
without its definitions/documents. **Inference:** a restriction, missed
condition and actionable enforcement event are different. An A/R access question
does not automatically establish this notes event of default. No actual default
or generic standstill period is inferred.

P12 has both ordinary consent flexibility and protected rights. The exact
**66⅔%** consent applies to the specified all-or-substantially-all collateral
release outside the document-permitted routes, or a materially adverse ICA
modification. It is not the vote for every release. Do not replace it with a
rounded decimal or **3%**. Ordinary majority flexibility does not override the
listed affected-holder payment/ranking protections; conversely, not every
transaction needs unanimity. The ICA still governs its own enforcement/control
matters; its body is unexamined here.

P13 suspends enumerated covenants only under the stated ratings/default
conditions, with reversion mechanics; it is not an unconditional permanent
waiver of the package. P14's defined control-change trigger can require a 101%
principal offer plus accrued interest, subject to its procedures, existing-credit
obligations and exceptions. That is conditional holder protection, not proof of
funds available to satisfy an offer. These provisions are monitoring inputs, not
evidence that the conditions have actually occurred.
